3. **Affiliate emails** — send 5 personalized ones this week. This is the highest-leverage slow burn.

## What NOT to do (from the research)

- Never drop a link as your first post in a group or subreddit — instant, permanent bans.
- Don't buy reviews or upvotes — sellers smell it and it poisons the brand.
- Don't run paid ads yet — at $29/mo with no funnel data, ads just burn money.
