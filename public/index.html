Beginner mistakes that kill new sellers

1. Buying inventory before doing the math.Run the Profit tab first. Most "great ideas" die at the fee math.
2. Ordering 1,000 units of product #1.Start with 200–300. A small live test teaches more than months of research.
3. Ignoring the listing.Great product + lazy listing = no sales. Photos and copy are half the battle.
4. Running out of stock in month two.Use the Inventory tab's reorder math from day one.
5. Buying reviews or using hacks.Account death. Never worth it.
6. Quitting at week 3.Listings take 60–90 days to find their level. Patience is a strategy.
